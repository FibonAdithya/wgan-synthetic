\vspace{-2em}

We trained several GANs on SIFT and looked at two kinds of similarity.  The
first is the structure of the original data, measured component by component.
The second is the distribution of nearest-neighbour relationships, which helps
determine how difficult the data is for approximate nearest-neighbour (ANN)
search.

Here we compare two models.  The \textbf{Dense GAN} is a simple neural network
trained with WGAN-GP.  The \textbf{Structured GAN}, our favourite model so far,
was designed for SIFT: it can produce exact zeros, model relationships between
SIFT components, and learn from local neighbourhoods.

The Dense GAN gives the closest match for LID.  This is surprising because it
was not trained to match that measure.  It is less convincing as SIFT data: it
produces no exact zeros, and almost 10\% of its values are negative.  Real SIFT
values are non-negative and about 23\% are exactly zero.

The Structured GAN closely matches these basic properties of real SIFT.  It is
also closer on hubness and IVF cell balance.  However, it is further away on
LID.  Its LID result also needs care: the model was trained using a closely
related neighbourhood measure, while the Dense GAN's result was not fitted in
this way.

Relative contrast does not separate the two models.  The Dense GAN sits
0.015 from real and the Structured GAN 0.059, but the 0.044 between them is
smaller than the 0.048 that this statistic moves when only the training seed
changes (\texttt{docs/datasets/sift.md}).
The other three ANN gaps above are larger than their respective floors.  All
figures are a single seed-7 draw with no error bars.

The two models therefore tell different stories.  The Dense GAN produces less
plausible SIFT vectors, but their local neighbourhood dimensionality is much
closer to real SIFT.  The Structured GAN produces much more plausible SIFT
vectors and is closer on hubness and IVF cell balance, but does not match
every measure of ANN difficulty as closely.

\section*{The question}

What matters more for this project: reproducing the original SIFT distribution,
or reproducing how difficult SIFT is for ANN search?

If the goal is to create realistic SIFT-like data, the Structured GAN is the
clearer choice.  If the goal is to create a realistic ANN workload, the Dense
GAN cannot be dismissed simply because its values look less like SIFT.  We may
instead want two separate tests: one for data fidelity and one for ANN
difficulty.
