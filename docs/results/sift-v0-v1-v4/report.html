Matched at 100k steps, the SIFT ladder does progress

v0, v1 and v4 at equal run length, read against real SIFT through three lenses. v4 wins on support and on half the gate — but v0 stays closest on relative contrast while being nowhere near SIFT’s support.

2026-08-13 · commit 2bf36eb · all rungs 100k generator steps · N=20000, k=100, hubness k=10, nlist=256, L2-normalized, seed 42

This supersedes an earlier 30k measurement, and reverses its headline. At 30k, v0 — plain WGAN-GP — was closer to real than v4 on all four gated statistics. At 100k it is closer on one.

The reason is that v4 was undertrained. Between 30k and 100k it closes 38–98% of each gate gap, against v0’s 15–28%. The regularized rung keeps learning; the plain baseline saturates early. Comparing the two at 30k measured the regularizer before it had paid off.

What survives from the earlier reading is narrower and still worth acting on: v0 sits within noise of real on relative contrast while producing 12.3% negative components and not one exact zero. Four statistics that measure neighbourhood geometry do not constrain support, so a rung can look good on some of them while being off-support — see what the gate does not see.

How the rungs were measured

One eda_report invocation over real sift_1m.npy and three overlays, at the canonical SIFT conditions locked in docs/datasets/sift.md. A single invocation is load-bearing: every rung shares one real-side subsample, so rung-to-rung differences carry no sampling noise of their own.

  • v0 — plain WGAN-GP. Trained for this report; the ladder had no 100k baseline.
  • v1 — runs/x100k_ema_only, + generator EMA.
  • v4 — v4_x100k, + structured gated generator + log-ratio regularizer.

All three are 100k generator steps, seed 42, on sift_1m.npy, same architecture. The v0 config is v1’s own config with ema_decay removed and nothing else changed, so v0 and v1 differ in exactly one key. The run-length confound present in the 30k version of this report is gone.

Distance from real, in units of noise

A gap only means something against how far the statistic moves when nothing but the seed changes. That floor is per architecture and does not transfer: the MLP rungs are judged against the v0 seed-to-seed floor from docs/datasets/sift.md, v4 against the architecture-matched v3-pair spread from docs/results/v4-logratio/.

No rung sweeps the gate. v4 takes hubness skew and IVF Gini, and its Gini is not merely closest but nearly exact — 0.3035 against real’s 0.3040, a gap of 0.0005, which is 0.04x its own noise floor. v0 keeps relative contrast at 0.11x the floor, indistinguishable from real. v1 takes LID.

v4’s LID is not evidence and ranks nothing. lid_reg fits the mean log-ratio profile of within-batch neighbours, the sufficient statistic the Hill estimator reduces to a scalar, so a good lid_median was guaranteed by construction — and in fact v4 is furthest out on it (14.5x). Discounting it leaves three usable statistics, split 2–1 in v4’s favour.

What run length changed

The 30k version of this report found v0 closer than v4 on all four. Both arms were 30k there, so that was not a confound — it was true at 30k. What it missed is how differently the two rungs respond to training.

Absolute gap to real at 30k → at 100k, and the fraction closed.

v4 closes 38–98% of every gap; v0 closes 15–28%. The two regularizers and the structured gate need well past 30k steps to express themselves, and the plain adversarial baseline has largely stopped moving by then. A ladder comparison at a run length short enough to leave the regularized rung undertrained will systematically favour the simpler rung. That is the methodological finding here, and it is worth more than either rung’s ranking.

Real SIFT descriptors are non-negative and quantized: 23.0% of coordinates are exactly zero, and 0.06% of rows are exact duplicates of another row. v0 and v1 produce zero exact zeros and put 12.3% and 9.8% of their mass below zero, down to −0.10. v4 never goes negative and matches the zero mass to 0.0012 — 0.2310 against real’s 0.2298, tighter than at 30k, where it overshot by 0.0143.

This is the one lens where the rungs are not close. It is not a ranking so much as a category difference: two rungs are off SIFT’s support entirely, and one is on it.

EMA alone does nothing

With run length matched, v0→v1 isolates generator EMA — one config key, all else equal. It moves nothing: LID 0.0622 (0.38x the floor), relative contrast 0.0095 (0.20x), hubness skew 0.0419 (0.68x), IVF Gini 0.0033 (0.47x). All four are under 1x — every one smaller than a reseed.

At 30k this question was confounded, because the ladder’s v1 was a 100k run. Matched, the answer is clean: on the gate, EMA is inert. It also does not help the support problem — v1 is as negative-heavy as v0.

What the lattice does to relative contrast

Relative contrast divides by the nearest-neighbour distance, and SIFT’s quantized lattice puts near-coincident pairs in the corpus, so a handful of real queries divide by almost zero and reach ~6.5×107. Those few points used to set the axis and collapse every curve into the first bin, leaving a panel that read as a rendering failure. It now bins to the 99.5th percentile and states what it left out — 130 of 79994 queries, 0.16%.

The tail is a property of the real corpus, not of the generators. Six real queries were dropped outright by survivor_mask as exact duplicates or full ties, and every synthetic rung dropped zero — no generator here produces a single exact duplicate or a fully tied neighbourhood. The gated median is computed from the full array and was never affected; only the histogram’s axis was.

What this does not say

  • The noise floors were measured on 30k runs. Both — the v0 seed floor and the v3-pair spread — come from 30k pairs, and every multiple on this page divides a 100k gap by one of them. A longer run need not have the same seed-to-seed spread. This is an extrapolation, and the claim it most affects is v0’s 0.11x on relative contrast. Measuring a 100k floor needs a second-seed 100k v0.
  • n=1 per rung. One seed each, and both floors are themselves n=2 — an order of magnitude and no more. Both are also lower bounds: two runs at identical seed still diverge 1–8% per loss column by step 250 from nondeterministic CUDA reduction order.
  • No gate band was set or moved. gates/sift.yaml is untouched and every band there is still null. Choosing a number is reserved for a human.
  • v4’s LID ranks nothing — fitted by construction, as above.
  • The real-vs-real floor is one split. It resolves “saturated” from “clearly above”, which is all it is used for. It does not support fine distinctions near 1x, and the ordering of v1 and v4 on the saturated diagnostics should not be read at all.
  • Run length is not a variant. Nothing here renames or repoints a rung. The 100k v0 is a new run of the existing v0 configuration, not a new rung.

What follows from it

Compare rungs at matched run length, and long enough for the regularizers to act. The 30k comparison inverted the ranking of the two most distant rungs on this ladder. 30k is not long enough to evaluate v4, and any future rung carrying a regularizer should be assumed to need the same headroom.

The gate still needs a support check. v0 sits within noise of real on relative contrast with 12.3% negative components and no exact zeros. That combination should not be able to look like a pass on any statistic. exact_zero_fraction and negative_fraction are already computed by eda_report. Whether that becomes a fifth gated statistic or a precondition is a banding decision, and belongs to a human.

Reproducing

Sampled vectors, the stock eda_report.html and the per-panel PNGs are on tig-gpu at /workspace/keep/sift-v0-v1-v4/eda100k/. The 100k v0 checkpoint is at /workspace/keep/sift-v0-v1-v4/v0_x100k/, beside the run_config.yaml it needs to be loadable. v1’s draw is the ladder’s existing samples_v1.npy, already a seed-42 draw from the 100k x100k_ema_only checkpoint.

# the missing arm: v0 at 100k, matching v1's config minus ema_decay
python -m src.train.train_wgan_gp --config v0_x100k_sift1m.yaml

python -m src.eval.eda_report     --real-path /workspace/data-cache/sift_1m.npy     --synthetic-path v0=samples_v0_100k.npy     --synthetic-path v1=samples_v1.npy     --synthetic-path v4=samples_v4_100k.npy     --output-dir eda100k --preprocess l2 --max-vectors 50000     --ann-max-rows 20000 --ann-k 100 --ann-hub-k 10 --ivf-nlist 256 --seed 42

The distribution diagnostics and their real-vs-real floor come from dist_diag.py, committed beside this page and run in the same job.
