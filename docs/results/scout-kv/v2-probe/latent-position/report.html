scout_kv v2 probe: latent and position inputs

Run on 2026-10-27 (commit f409ae6, tig-gpu-alt L40). The two-critic run left two questions. Does the drifting latent have to cost 2.5–5.5 points of body LID to keep book clustering? Do the sinusoidal position features make L19 search too easy? Both variants below are Bboth (v1's per-token critic plus the window critic at weight 0.1) with one change each. Runs are at layers 27 and 19, seeds 42 and 1, for 30k steps. All numbers are MEASURED.

  • Bflag: the tail bit is the only position input, as in v1.
  • Bsmall: 16 latent channels instead of 64, and length scales of 2–1,024 tokens instead of 2–16,384.

Both trained normally. At the final step the window W-distance is 43–51 for Bflag and 38–43 for Bsmall at L27 (Bboth 28–33), with gradient penalties of 1.1–1.5. That is far from the ablation's Cflag, whose window critic won outright (W-distance ~625). The window term's per-token gradient is 0.38–0.55× the token term's.

Findings

  1. The position features do not make L19 search too easy. This retracts the two-critic report's reading. Bflag's L19 HNSW recall is 0.989 / 0.969 against a real range of 0.854–0.882, no better than Bboth (0.980 / 0.976). Every probe run has a window critic and is too easy at L19. v0 has no window critic and is too easy as well (0.972). v1 is the only generator near real (0.869–0.902). The cause is not identified.
  2. Removing the position features costs nothing. Bflag's body LID is 21.02 / 20.35 at L27 (Bboth 19.62 / 19.04) and 23.12 / 23.14 at L19 (Bboth 22.94 / 23.77). Its h-NN same-book share is 0.935 / 0.902 at L27 (real 0.895) and 0.761 / 0.698 at L19 (real 0.645). The latent alone carries the book structure. Without position input, its tokens are tied to each other only through the latent. At L27 the seeds disagree on how tightly: lag-256 cosine 0.379 at seed 42 and 0.565 at seed 1, against 0.498 within a real book and 0.430 between real books.
  3. The short latent recovers most of the LID but loses the book clustering. Bsmall's body LID is 22.59 / 23.07 at L27 and 24.54 / 25.19 at L19, 0.5–2.5 below Cboth. Its h-NN same-book share falls to 0.644 / 0.575 at L27 (real 0.895) and 0.333 / 0.229 at L19 (real 0.645). Key-NN same-book falls with it (L27 0.347 / 0.292 vs real 0.653). Book identity lives in the latent's slow components, the ones longer than 1,024 tokens.
  4. Hubness stays far from real. Real is 49.9 and 52.3. Bflag is 3.2–3.5, except one run at 8.5 (L19, seed 1). Bsmall is 4.1–6.5, near v1's 5.5–7.3.
  5. No run lands inside the real range on most gate statistics. At L27, Bflag seed 42 is too easy (HNSW recall 0.905, IVF recall 0.937) and seed 1 too hard (0.751, IVF ratio 0.656 vs real 1.776–1.866). The spread between seeds is as large as before.

Reading for v2

The trade-off is between LID and book clustering, and it sits in the latent's long length scales. A shorter latent buys LID by giving up the clustering. Position features are not needed: the tail bit is enough, as in v1. The too-easy L19 search has a cause outside both changes tested here.
