scout_kv v0 against the real reference

One WGAN-GP generator per Llama 4 Scout global layer (12 layers) samples the attention input h. Keys and queries are projected through the real W_k and W_q of each KV head, giving 96 corpora, the same as the real reference. Each is searched with IVF and HNSW under the canonical conditions in gates/scout_kv.yaml. Every band in that file is null, so the verdict is unset for both checkpoints. This page puts the v0 values next to the noise floor of the real reference so that bands can be chosen.

What the numbers show

Gate statistics, v0 best checkpoint against the five real draws:

  • HNSW reach, k=10: v0 best 0.8750 is above the real range 0.5625-0.6250.
  • HNSW recall@10 at ef 176: v0 best 0.9274 is above the real range 0.7982-0.8526.
  • IVF scan ratio, k=128 (ood / id): v0 best 0.6841 is below the real range 0.9403-1.0761.
  • IVF recall@128 at nprobe 24: v0 best 0.8246 is inside the real range 0.8185-0.8432.
  • HNSW recall@10 at ef 176, key-side queries: v0 best 0.9757 is below the real range 1.0000-1.0000.

On out-of-distribution queries, HNSW finds v0 corpora easier than real ones, and IVF scans a smaller share of them relative to key-side queries. IVF recall at the operating point matches. On key-side queries, v0 is harder than real.

Intrinsic statistics of h (diagnostics, one subsample each):

  • Hubness skew: real 38.3-47.2 at layers 15-47 and 2.7-29.2 at layers 3-11; v0 best 4.0-14.4 across all layers. v0 does not reproduce the hubs of real h.
  • IVF Gini at layers 15-47: real 0.529-0.768, v0 best 0.382-0.566. v0 fills k-means cells more evenly.
  • LID median: v0 best is lower than real at layers 15, 19, 23, 27, 31, 35, 39, 43, 47 and higher at the rest.

These are descriptions of the measured values, not explanations. No cause has been tested.
