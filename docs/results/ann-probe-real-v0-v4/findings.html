Read this first. All three corpora are 1,000,000 × 128, L2-normalized, because the generators only emit unit-norm vectors. These figures do not describe raw SIFT1M and are not comparable with published results. v0 and v4 are both 100k-step runs on sift_1m (v0 trained 2026-09-23, v4 the admitted 2026-09-17 retrain). Queries: 10,000 per corpus. Real uses SIFT's own query set; synthetic queries are a separate draw from each generator. Ground truth is exact k=10. This is one run. The only measurement of run-to-run noise is the 2026-08-13 repeat on a different card (RTX 4060): recall moved ≤ 0.005 and QPS ≤ 4.6% at a fixed setting. That floor was not re-measured on this card.

Findings

  • Build time does not tell the corpora apart. IVF-Flat 1.33–1.34 s, IVF-PQ 2.93–3.05 s, CAGRA 4.58–5.08 s. Real's CAGRA build is 0.5 s slower than the others. Real was built first in the process, so this may be warm-up rather than data, but that was not tested.
  • At a fixed search setting, QPS is the same on all three corpora (max/min spread ≤ 3% at 38 of 39 settings, 4.3% at the worst). Search cost follows the knob, not the data. The corpora differ only in the recall that cost buys.
  • v0 tracks real. At QPS for recall 0.90 and 0.95, v0/real is 0.98–1.02 on IVF-Flat and CAGRA. The one visible gap is at very low IVF probe counts: n_probes=1 gives recall 0.251 against 0.271. That is about 4× the August recall noise, and it closes by n_probes=16.
  • v4 is easier to search than real, on both families. At equal settings it has higher recall: CAGRA max_iterations=20 gives 0.934 against 0.901, and IVF-Flat n_probes=8 gives 0.724 against 0.709. Its CAGRA recall ceiling is 0.982 against 0.966. As QPS at a matched recall, v4 is 1.10× real on IVF-Flat at 0.90 and 1.14× at 0.95. On CAGRA (max_iterations sweep) it is 1.19× at 0.90 and 1.30× at 0.95.
  • IVF-PQ never reaches 0.90 on any corpus (peaks 0.879–0.886). That limit comes from the pq_dim=64 compression, not from the data.
  • The CAGRA itopk rows are not a comparison. The cheapest setting (itopk=32) already exceeds 0.96 on every corpus, so the table shows each corpus's floor at a different recall.
