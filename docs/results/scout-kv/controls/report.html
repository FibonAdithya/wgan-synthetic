scout_kv controls: why v0 looks too easy

Box run B7 on tig-gpu-alt (L40), 2026-10-19. Every number on this page is MEASURED unless marked ESTIMATE. Sources: summary.json, built by summarise.py from the JSONL files in this directory.

R1 (hard stop): the gate's measurement path flatters synthetic data. Per seed: pooled_real: fires, pooled_real_seed1: fires, pooled_real_seed2: fires. Real h from the reference books, split into keys and queries the way synthetic corpora are (one pool, no position), is already outside the real range in v0's direction for HNSW recall@10 on at least 2 of 3 layers and for the IVF ratio on all 3, at every seed. Under the plan's rules, v1 is not designed; the gate question goes to the owner.

R2: does not fire (the training books 0-7 are about as hard as the reference books). v0 re-measured on the L40: matches the committed records within the tolerance fixed before the run.

R3 and R4 (descriptive)

  • R3, Gaussian fit: at least as far from real as v0, in v0's direction, on 8 of the 9 rule cells; the exception is layer 19's IVF ratio (0.097 against v0's 0.095). At layer 43 the gap is large: HNSW reach 1.000 against v0's 0.375 and real 0.000-0.125. Second-order structure alone is mostly easier than v0, so v0 has learned something beyond the covariance.
  • R4, norm-equalised: it does not move pooled_real toward v0. Removing the spread of norms makes HNSW much harder (recall@10 at layer 43: 0.296 against pooled 0.770 and real 0.574-0.637), so norm spread makes real search easier, not harder.

Descriptive, not a rule result. Same and cross share their key rows within a draw, so they can be paired. At layers 11 and 43, cross minus same is at most 0.045 in recall@10 and 0.08 in IVF ratio, far below the spread across draws (layer 43 IVF ratio 3.13-3.71). At layer 43 both tail controls keep an IVF ratio of 3.10-3.71, against 1.12-1.15 for pooled_real. Layer 19, the layer with the strongest same-book recency, differs more (up to 0.061 in recall and 0.092 in IVF ratio), with the sign set by the partition. Why R6 fails: with four key books, layer 19 depends on which books form the key set.

R6a, R6 and R7: held-out-book control

The rule-grade redo of the cross-book control. Descriptive, not rule results. Held-out queries are harder than the rebuild at layer 19 in every draw (recall@10 down 0.047-0.091, IVF ratio up 0.095-0.171). Books 0-7 measured alone are also harder than the reference at layer 19, so at least part of that gap is a difference between books 0-11 and 12-19, which the rule cannot separate from an own-book effect. Layer 11 does not depend on the query's book (|Δ recall| ≤ 0.014). At layer 43 held-out queries keep and add to the reference's IVF hardness (IVF ratio 3.32-4.05 against rebuild 2.96-3.55). The five-draw real range is narrow: the rebuild, exchangeable with the reference draws, falls outside it in 17 of 36 cells. Three of the 12 layers; the 96-corpus gate was not run.

All 5,120 real-style queries at layer 3 share 11 target keys of 31,736. Those keys are not simply the longest (norm-count correlation about 0). Over all 8 heads, the rerun's median is 31-949 distinct keys per layer (table above). At 8 of the 9 layers from 15 on, those keys sit at median positions 26,609-30,087, late in the books. The same-book share stays near 1/8 at most of these layers, so the late keys come from any book. The distinct-key count was not part of the pre-registered records; it is reported here as an observation and a candidate statistic for follow-up, not a result of this plan.

Caveats

  • Each control except pooled_real is a single draw (seed 0) against the range of five real draws. Layer 11's pooled_real changes status across seeds on reach and recall@10; layers 19 and 43 do not.
  • The v0 match tolerances (reach ±1/8, recalls ±0.01, IVF ratio ±5% relative) are ESTIMATE (unverified) judgements fixed before the run, not measured noise.
  • Layers 11, 19 and 43 only for the controls; the neighbour diagnostic covers all 12.
