The SIFT gate ranks the weakest generator first

v0, v1 and v4 read against real SIFT in one measurement — and what four aggregate statistics fail to see.

2026-08-13 · commit 2bf36eb · N=20000, k=100, hubness k=10, nlist=256, L2-normalized, seed 42

v0 — plain WGAN-GP, no EMA, no gated generator, no regularizer — sits closer to real SIFT than v4 on all four gate statistics. On relative contrast it is 0.13x the seed-to-seed noise floor away from real: indistinguishable.

It does this while emitting 11.8% negative components and not one exact zero, on a corpus whose defining structural fact is heavy mass at exactly zero. v4 is the only rung that reproduces that support, and it is the rung the gate ranks last.

This is a result about the gate, not an argument for shipping v0. The four statistics in src/eval/ann_difficulty.py measure neighbourhood geometry. They do not constrain support, so a generator can reproduce the geometry while placing its mass where SIFT descriptors cannot exist.

How the rungs were measured

One eda_report invocation over real sift_1m.npy and four overlays, at the canonical SIFT conditions locked in docs/datasets/sift.md. A single invocation is load-bearing: every rung shares one real-side subsample, so rung-to-rung differences carry no sampling noise of their own.

  • v0 — runs/long_baseline, 30k steps, plain WGAN-GP.
  • v1 — runs/x100k_ema_only, 100k steps, + generator EMA. This is the rung the ladder defines.
  • v1_30k — runs/long_ema_only, 30k steps, + EMA. A control, not a ladder rung: v1 is 3.3x longer than v0, so the ladder’s own v0→v1 step confounds EMA with run length. This separates them.
  • v4 — 30k steps, structured gated generator + log-ratio regularizer.

Distance from real, in units of noise

A gap only means something against how far the statistic moves when nothing but the seed changes. That floor is per architecture and does not transfer: the MLP rungs are judged against the v0 seed-to-seed floor from docs/datasets/sift.md, v4 against the architecture-matched v3-pair spread from docs/results/v4-logratio/.

v4’s LID number is not evidence and is not used here to rank anything. lid_reg fits the mean log-ratio profile of within-batch neighbours, which is the sufficient statistic the Hill estimator reduces to a scalar. A good lid_median was guaranteed by construction. Its 23.6x gap is reported for completeness only.

Real SIFT descriptors are non-negative and quantized: 23.0% of coordinates are exactly zero, and 0.06% of rows are exact duplicates of another row. v0, v1 and v1_30k produce zero exact zeros and put roughly 10–12% of their mass below zero, down to −0.19. v4 reproduces the zero mass to within 0.014 and never goes negative.

What the EMA rung actually bought

The ladder’s v0→v1 step looks like nothing: every statistic moves under 1.8x the floor, three of them under 1x. The control shows that this is not because EMA does nothing, but because two effects of similar size cancel.

Adding EMA at matched run length moves LID and hubness skew about 1.6–2.0x the floor toward real. Extending 30k→100k moves both back out again by about the same amount. The net is a wash, and v1_30k — a rung that does not exist on the ladder — is the closest of any rung to real on hubness skew (1.35x floor).

None of these deltas is large. At 2x an n=2 floor, the honest reading is “possibly real, not established”.

A panel that cannot be read, for a real reason

The relative-contrast histogram renders with its x-axis running to about 6.5×107 and every bar in the first bin. The cause is in the real set: relative contrast divides by the nearest-neighbour distance, and SIFT’s quantized lattice puts near-coincident pairs in the corpus, so a handful of real queries divide by almost zero.

Six real queries were dropped outright by survivor_mask as exact duplicates or full ties. Every synthetic rung dropped zero — no generator here produces a single exact duplicate or a fully tied neighbourhood. The median the gate uses is unaffected; only the histogram is. It is worth an issue against the panel, and it is one more instance of the report’s theme: the lattice structure is what the generators are missing.

What this does not say

  • It does not say v0 is the better generator. It says four aggregate statistics do not constrain support, so a rung can win them while being obviously wrong under a panel that looks at individual vectors.
  • No gate band was set or moved. gates/sift.yaml is untouched and every band there is still null. Choosing a number is a judgement call reserved for a human.
  • IVF Gini ranks nothing. The architecture-matched floor makes it the weakest of the four discriminators, as docs/results/v4-logratio/ already found for the v3/v4 pair.
  • n=1 per rung. One seed each. Both noise floors are themselves n=2, establishing an order of magnitude and no more, and both are lower bounds — two runs at identical seed still diverge 1–8% per loss column by step 250 from nondeterministic CUDA reduction order.
  • Nothing here renames or repoints a variant. v1_30k is a control.

What follows from it

The gate needs a support check sitting beside the four statistics — exact-zero fraction and negative fraction are already computed by eda_report and would have caught v0 immediately. Whether that becomes a fifth gated statistic or a precondition is a banding decision, and belongs to a human.

Reproducing

Sampled vectors, the stock 8.5MB eda_report.html and the per-panel PNGs are on tig-gpu at /workspace/keep/sift-v0-v1-v4/. The v0, v1 and v4 draws are reused verbatim from /workspace/keep/sift-ladder/; only the v1_30k control was newly sampled.

python -m src.sample.generate \
    --checkpoint /workspace/keep/wgan-synthetic/long_ema_only/best_generator.pt \
    --config v1_30k_cpu.yaml --num-samples 20000 --seed 42 \
    --output-path samples_v1_30k.npy

python -m src.eval.eda_report \
    --real-path /workspace/data-cache/sift_1m.npy \
    --synthetic-path v0=samples_v0.npy \
    --synthetic-path v1=samples_v1.npy \
    --synthetic-path v1_30k=samples_v1_30k.npy \
    --synthetic-path v4=samples_v4.npy \
    --output-dir eda --preprocess l2 --max-vectors 50000 \
    --ann-max-rows 20000 --ann-k 100 --ann-hub-k 10 --ivf-nlist 256 --seed 42
