The result in one minute

The project succeeds only when synthetic vectors reproduce how hard real SIFT is to search. Distribution similarity is supporting evidence, not the decision rule.

Architecture
Structure matters

The MLP cannot emit exact zeros. Independent gates add zeros but miss SIFT's correlated 4×4×8 support. Structured gates model that support directly.

100k-step comparison
No simple winner

v1_5 is closest on LID and contrast, v2 on hubness, and v4 on IVF balance. The dense models score well while still missing SIFT's exact-zero support.

Operational cost at 1M
1.44 s p95

Structured-gated generation plus host transfer costs 1.441 s p95. That is 28% above the MLP's 1.128 s budget, with only ~54 MiB peak generation VRAM.

Do not declare a winner or a pass. SIFT gate bands are still unset, each row is one trained run, and the metrics disagree. The 100k ladder reveals the core tradeoff: dense MLPs can look close on ANN aggregates while being structurally incapable of reproducing SIFT's support.

Closest by metric
Split decision

v1_5 leads LID and contrast; v2 leads hubness; v4 leads IVF balance. No generator dominates the four-dimensional gate.

Architecture caveat
Dense can flatter

v1/v1_5 cannot emit exact zeros. Their close ANN aggregates do not mean they reproduce SIFT's support; support diagnostics explain the mismatch.

Objective caveat
v4 LID is fitted

v4 trains on LID's sufficient statistic. Its LID movement confirms the penalty worked, but contrast, hubness, and Gini carry the independent evidence.

Why this is now comparable: every displayed checkpoint trained for 100k generator steps, every sample set has 20,000 rows drawn at seed 7, and all series were passed to one evaluation invocation. This controls run length, sampling, real subsample, and evaluation settings—not training-seed uncertainty (n=1 per variant).

Recommendation to the team

Choose based on the challenge objective: reproducing ANN difficulty under a per-instance runtime deadline.

Promote for further validation
v2 + v4

v2 is the lean exact-zero baseline and leads hubness; v4 is the most expressive and leads IVF balance. Carry both into the seed sweep rather than choosing from one run.

Next measurement
Matched sweep

Sample v1, v2, v3, and v4 together under canonical conditions, then run 3–5 training seeds for the finalists before setting bands.

Runtime design
Keep it on GPU

If the index can consume GPU tensors, avoid the ~0.59–0.60 s host phase at 1M. Reuse the process to amortize CUDA initialization.
