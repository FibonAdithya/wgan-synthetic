Run on 2026-10-27 (commit 7460b2e, tig-gpu-alt L40). The ablation found that the 8-token window critic cost LID and that v1's per-token critic restored it. This run trains both critics side by side, each with its own optimizer and gradient penalty. The generator loss is the token score plus 0.1 × the window score. Arms are B (drifting per-book latent) and C (position only), each as Bboth and Cboth, at layers 27 and 19 with seeds 42 and 1, for 30k steps. Rows B, C and Ctok are earlier single-critic runs. All numbers are MEASURED.

At the final step, the window term's per-token gradient on the fakes is 0.35–0.50× the token term's (grad_win / grad_tok in train_log.jsonl). So the window critic still contributes, without dominating.

Findings

  1. With two critics, the position-only generator keeps v1's spread. Cboth's body LID is 25.10 / 25.26 at L27 (v1 25.03) and 25.66 / 25.65 at L19 (v1 25.98). Contrast also matches: 1.98 / 1.97 against v1's 1.98 at L27, and 1.89 / 1.89 against 1.88 at L19. The window term at weight 0.1 therefore costs no LID.
  2. The drifting latent still costs LID, now on its own account. Bboth's body LID is 19.62 / 19.05 at L27 and 22.94 / 23.77 at L19. That is above B with the window critic alone (16.19 / 16.67 and 20.05 / 19.79), but 5.5 and 2.5 below Cboth, which differs only by the latent. Real is 29.96 and 29.25.
  3. Bboth keeps B's book clustering in h space. Its h-NN same-book share at L27 is 0.889 / 0.900 (real 0.895, B 0.909 / 0.904). At L19 it is 0.744 / 0.532 (real 0.645, B 0.729 / 0.656), where the two seeds disagree. In key space it is lower than B's: 0.405 / 0.499 at L27 (real 0.653, B 0.517 / 0.520) and 0.266 / 0.201 at L19 (real 0.327).
  4. Body hubness stays far from real. Real is 49.9 and 52.3. Bboth is 2.7–4.0 and Cboth is 4.2–4.9, except one run, Cboth seed 42 at L27, at 16.8. v1 is 5.5–7.3.
  5. Search does not improve, and at L19 the position input looks like the problem. At L19, every run with position features makes HNSW recall too easy. Bboth scores 0.980 / 0.976, Cboth 0.941 / 0.965, B 0.965 / 0.949 and C 0.984 / 0.979, against real 0.854–0.882. v1, which has only the tail flag, scores 0.869–0.902. At L27, Bboth's IVF ratio is 0.658 at seed 42 and 1.434 at seed 1, against real 1.776–1.866. The same spread between seeds as before.
  6. Tokens are still tied to position more tightly than in real books. At lag 256, Cboth's cosine is 0.539 / 0.538 at L27 and 0.438 / 0.436 at L19. Between different real books it is 0.430 and 0.329.

Reading for v2

v1's per-token critic plus a down-weighted window critic is a usable critic: it keeps v1's spread and still trains the window structure. The drifting latent as built, with 64 channels and length scales from 2 to 16,384 tokens, buys the book clustering at 2.5–5.5 points of body LID. The sinusoidal position features likely make L19 recall too easy and overstate how much position decides a token. Ctok was not run at L19, so the second point rests on comparing against v1, which has no position features.
